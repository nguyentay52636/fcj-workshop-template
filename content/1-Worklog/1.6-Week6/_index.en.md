- Prevent the system from running "blind" — detect incidents immediately, distinguish between minor and critical errors, and route alerts to the right channel (Slack) for timely operational response.

### Week 6 Achievements:

- **Automated Monitoring & Alerting System:** The highlight of this week is that the system can now "self-report" when issues occur — no manual log watching required. Categorizing alarms into Warning/Critical levels avoids "alarm fatigue": minor/temporary errors go to the info channel, while user-impacting issues (DLQ backlog, Bedrock throttling) trigger immediate Slack alerts (`#rag-alerts`).

- **Flow 4 (RAGAS) designed as Partial:** Terraform modules, IAM shape, EventBridge schedule design, and a skeleton `evaluation_runner` were put in place. This is **not** yet claimed as a fully automated daily scoring loop — live ECR image push, Lambda invoke, and S3 result evidence remain follow-up work documented in [5.6.4](../5-Workshop/5.6-RAGAS/5.6.4-Testing-Deployment-Notes/).

- **Flows 1–3 operational; Flow 4 architectural:** By week end, Flow 3 (Monitoring/Alert) was automated end-to-end. Flow 4 completed the **architecture chapter** of the four-flow design, with honest Partial status on runtime evaluation.
